🟡 To & Half (:30 - :55)

💡 Summary of Time Rules:

:05 - :25 → [minutes] past [Hour] :30 → half past [Hour] :35 - :55 → [minutes] to [Next Hour] Digital Style: [Hour] + [Minutes] (e.g. 12:05 = twelve oh-five)

Always use preposition "at" with specific times: "The train leaves at ten past eight (8:10)."

🗣️ Partner Speaking Instructions:
1. Do not show your screen to your partner.
2. Look at your times (numbers only) and say them in English words to your partner.
3. Ask: "What time does Sam [activity]?" • Answer: "Sam [activity]s at [time in words]."
4. Important: Type your partner's answers in words only (e.g. "half past seven" or "seven thirty"). Numbers (e.g. "7:30") will not be accepted!
